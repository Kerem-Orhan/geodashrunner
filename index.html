Savefile Runner

Play Geometry Dash levels from your own save file in the browser. Everything is read from files on your computer, inside this tab.

Add your files

  1. Your saved levels

    CCGameManager.dat holds every level you've downloaded. A single-level .gmd export also works.

    %LOCALAPPDATA%\GeometryDash

  2. The level's song

    The numbered .mp3 or .ogg in the same folder. The player tells you which number once you pick a level.

  3. Game art optional

    Each GJ_GameSheet .png with its matching .plist (01–04, any quality). Without them, levels draw as simple shapes.

    Steam\steamapps\common\Geometry Dash\Resources

Drop the two folders here, or

Add the GeometryDash folder first, then the game's Resources folder. Only the files the player needs are read.

No files yet.

Files never leave your computer: decoding, music, and art all happen in this tab, and closing it forgets them.
